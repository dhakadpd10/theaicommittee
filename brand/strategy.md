# Strategy for the week of 2026-10-30 (growth reset)

Written by hand on 29 Oct after the account audit. The Saturday audit replaces it from next week.

## Why we changed again
22 reels, about 30,000 views, 6 followers, 2 comments. Every reel stopped at Instagram's first test group (1,500 to 2,200 views) because almost nobody shared or commented. The first reels of the new format did not help: TRUTH (same prompt) 1,224 views, 1 like, 0 comments; Mum asks part 1, 204 views. The pages that grew fast did it with a painful, shared problem plus a real free fix and a keyword worth commenting (@ashutoshwithclaude 25K: 2,500 to 6,800 comments per hit). Pages that only copied the animated look got nowhere.

## Make more of
- Real problems with stakes: jobs and rejections, salary, late-night bosses, scams aimed at parents.
- A fix that really works, shown on the laptop as 2 to 4 steps, with the full version (official link, steps, prompts) by keyword.
- Hooks with a number and tension: "87 applications. 0 interviews."

## Stop
- "Same prompt, 3 AIs" (retired).
- Quiz hooks ("I asked 3 AIs...") and low-stakes everyday jokes.
- Two reels a day: each one splits the same small test group and we can't tell which reel brought followers.

## Length and endings
20 to 30 seconds. People watched about 15 s of 28 s reels.
