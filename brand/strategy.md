# Strategy for the week of 2026-11-01 (new format)

Written by hand on 28 Oct after the growth review. The Saturday audit replaces it from next week.

## Why we changed
22 reels, about 33,000 views, 6 followers, 1 comment, about 22 shares. People watched (15 s of about 28 s), but nothing made them comment, send or follow. Every reel had the same skeleton (Hey committee → Claude asks about feelings → Gemini "Done! I also..." → Codex literal → twin tag), so after two reels the joke was predictable, and most jokes were random disasters, not things real AIs do.

## Make more of
- Jokes built on true AI behaviour. Our best views were the truest: 003 (confident, wrong) and 011 (Claude over-apologising).
- Series with parts and days, so there is a reason to follow.
- Useful moments: the Mum asks answer must really teach the word.

## Stop
- The "Hey committee" opener and the fixed speaking order.
- Random disasters with no real-life source.
- Story questions in captions. Ask for one word, one pick or one vote.

## Hooks
Every reel opens with a big on-screen hook for 3 s that says the test ("I told 3 AIs that 2 + 2 = 5"). Compare hook styles next week: "I asked 3 AIs..." vs "Mum asked me..." vs "Day N:".

## One experiment
Score each series on comments + shares + saves per 1,000 reach after 7 days. A series that is under half of the best one after 4 posts gets replaced by a new series test.
