# Idea bank

Everyday problems Meera can bring to the committee. The planner picks from here, adds fresh ones from research, and never repeats a topic from the last 4 weeks.

- **Work:** the leave email (done), clear my calendar (done), the meeting summary ("nobody decided anything; I said you'd present"), reply-all, the performance review, asking for a raise, the out-of-office message, "make this sound confident", the 9 am standup, the resignation draft, LinkedIn post about a small win.
- **Daily life:** will it rain (done), what should I cook (Mum on the phone says dal), plan my weekend, the grocery list, splitting the bill with friends, the gym plan, "help me sleep earlier", the electricity bill, booking a train ticket.
- **Family:** reply to Mum's good-morning forward, the family group chat, explaining my job to my grandmother, the wedding RSVP, birthday wish for a relative whose name nobody remembers.
- **Dating and friends:** "reply to Rohan, sound casual" (Codex: "yo. regards, Meera."), the birthday message, cancelling plans politely, the "are you mad at me" text.
- **Money:** the budget, "can I afford this sale", the refund request, haggling.
- **Her side business (Mitti candles):** the discount code that leaks, the influencer who wants 40 free candles, the 1-star review reply, the Diwali sale banner, pricing.
- **Seasonal:** Diwali, exam results, monsoon, New Year resolutions, Valentine's Day, tax day, cricket final.
